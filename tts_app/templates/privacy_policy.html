Data Protection

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 13, 2026 • Compliant with GDPR, CCPA, and Google AdSense Policies

1. Introduction & Privacy Commitment

Welcome to Script2Voice Studio ("we," "our," or "us"). We are committed to protecting the privacy and confidentiality of our users in the United States, United Kingdom, European Union, and worldwide. This Privacy Policy explains how our Text to Speech web application handles user data, browser storage, and temporary media files.

2. Zero Database Tracking & Browser LocalStorage

We operate on a Stateless & Privacy-First Architecture. We do NOT store your text inputs, audio scripts, or generated conversion history in any server-side database. All conversion history items are stored exclusively within your own web browser's localStorage.

  • Your generated audio history remains 100% private to your browser.
  • No other visitor or third party can access your saved audio conversions.
  • You can clear your local audio history at any time by clicking the delete button or clearing your browser cache.

3. Temporary Media File Management

When you convert text into speech, our backend synthesizes a temporary .mp3 file to deliver the audio stream to your browser. These temporary files are stored in a transient server directory and are automatically purged and deleted after 2 hours by our background janitor routine. We do not archive or store your spoken audio files permanently on our servers.

4. Advertising & Google AdSense Cookies

We use Google AdSense to serve advertisements on our web application. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to our website or other websites.

  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to users based on their visit to our sites and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • Users in the United States and European Union may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ad Settings.

5. Compliance with EU GDPR & California CCPA

Because we do not store personally identifiable information (PII) or user accounts on our servers, you can use Script2Voice with complete anonymity. Under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), you retain full ownership and control over your locally stored browser data.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy or data privacy practices, please contact us at privacy@script2voice.com.
